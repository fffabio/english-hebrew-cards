      Ella’s Secret Invention

      Ella is thirteen. She loves science, and she wants to be an engineer like her aunt Dana. Dana lives in Rome, the capital of Italy, and she builds bridges.

      Every afternoon, Ella goes to her room and works on a secret invention. She doesn’t tell her friends about it, and her little brother doesn’t know about it either. Only her grandfather knows. He is an expert on machines, and he helps her a lot.

      What is the invention? It is a small robot for the garden. Ella’s mother grows vegetables, but insects often eat her carrots. The robot has a special sense of smell, so it finds the insects and sprays them with water. It doesn’t hurt them. It only sends them away.

      The robot isn’t perfect yet. It is made of iron, so it is very heavy, and it doesn’t move fast. Ella tries a new idea every week, and she watches videos online to learn more.

      Right now, Ella is sitting at her desk. She is fixing the robot’s legs, and her grandfather is drinking tea next to her. They are both smiling. Maybe this time it works!
