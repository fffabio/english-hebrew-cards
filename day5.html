Present simple or present progressive?

Present simplehabits and facts: She goes shopping every Friday.
always, usually, often, sometimes, never, every day
Present progressivehappening now: Look! She is taking photos.
now, right now, at the moment, Look!, Listen!
No -inglike, love, want, know, need, understand: I want a camera. (not “I am wanting”)
הרגל או עובדה → present simple. קורה עכשיו → present progressive. פעלים של רצון וידיעה (want, know, like, need) – בלי ing, גם עכשיו.

1–10: choose the correct form. 11–16: write the words in brackets in the right form. 1–10: בחרי את הצורה הנכונה. 11–16: כתבי את המילים שבסוגריים בצורה הנכונה.

      A Lucky Day at the Mall

      Every Friday, Shira and her mom go shopping at the mall. Shira loves clothes, but her mom usually refuses to buy her expensive things. “Clothes aren’t essential,” she says. “Food is essential!”

      This Friday is different. There is a big sign at the entrance: “Answer three questions and win a digital camera!” There are plenty of people in line, but despite the long line, Shira wants to try. “I never miss a chance!” she says.

      The questions are not easy, but Shira is positive that she knows the answers. Her mom isn’t so sure. “I think you’re wrong about the second one,” she says. But somehow, Shira gets all three answers right.

      “Congratulations!” says the man at the table. “You’re very lucky. Here is your camera.” The rules are clear: the winner doesn’t pay anything, so Shira’s mom doesn’t need her credit card.

      Right now, Shira is walking around the mall and taking photos of everything. Her mom is laughing. “Maybe Fridays aren’t so bad,” she says.
