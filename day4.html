      The Phone Deal

      Every month, Noam’s mother checks the family phone bill. This month she isn’t happy. “They charge us too much!” she says. She wants a new phone company.

      Noam is fifteen, and he loves technology, so he helps her. He finds an advertisement on a website: “Great deal! Fast internet for 30 shekels a month. No fee for the first year!”

      “It sounds good,” says his mother, “but do they lie? Some companies offer a low price, and then the price goes up.” So Noam reads the contract carefully. He has experience with long texts, because he reads a lot. His favorite subject at school is literature!

      At first, his mother doesn’t want to change. But Noam shows her the numbers and convinces her. In the end, she agrees.

      Right now, Noam’s mother is smiling, and there is a tear in her eye. It’s a happy tear. She is looking at the first bill from the new company, and it is half the old price!
