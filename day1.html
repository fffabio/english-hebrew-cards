Noa’s blog

9 new words, present simple, present progressive, and one short text

כ-35 דקות. עובדים לפי הסדר: מילים, דקדוק, קריאה.

1

New words

Say the word, try to remember the Hebrew, then tap to check. Write one sentence with each word. תגידי את המילה, נסי לזכור את התרגום, ואז לחצי לבדוק. כתבי משפט אחד עם כל מילה.

2

Present simple: he / she / it + s

I / you / we / theyplay, write, live
he / she / itplays, writes, lives
כשהנושא הוא הוא / היא / זה (או שם של אדם אחד, כמו Noa או my cousin) מוסיפים s לפועל.

1–10: choose the correct verb. 11–18: write the verb in brackets in the right form. 1–10: בחרי את הצורה הנכונה. 11–18: כתבי את הפועל שבסוגריים בצורה הנכונה.

    3

    Present progressive: am / is / are + -ing

    Iam reading
    he / she / itis reading
    you / we / theyare reading
    Spellingmake → making, swim → swimming, play → playing
    Signal wordsnow, right now, at the moment, Look!, Listen!
    Present progressive = משהו שקורה עכשיו, ברגע זה. Present simple = משהו שקורה תמיד / כל יום / בדרך כלל.

    1–6: choose am, is or are. 7–10: present simple or present progressive? 11–16: write the verb in brackets with am/is/are + -ing. 1–6: בחרי am, is או are. 7–10: הווה פשוט או הווה מתמשך? 11–16: כתבי את הפועל שבסוגריים בהווה מתמשך.

      4

      Read and answer

      Read the text, then answer the questions. The new words are in yellow. קראי את הקטע וענו על השאלות. המילים החדשות מסומנות בצהוב.

      My Cousin’s Blog

      My cousin Noa is fourteen. She lives in a small town in the north. Her father works in a chocolate factory, and Noa writes a blog about food. Every week she tries a new dish and writes about its taste. Her readers love the blog. They say her photos are amazing.

      Every Friday, Noa’s father brings home chocolate from the factory. It is delicious! But Noa says that cooking is part of the fun, so she usually makes her own desserts.

      Noa’s best friend, Maya, loves cooking too. She comes to Noa’s house every Tuesday after school, and the two girls make cookies together. Maya takes the photos, and Noa writes the posts. Their cookies taste great, and the blog gets more readers every month.

      Sometimes Noa gets upset. Some people copy her photos and put them on their websites without asking. That is illegal! Her father tells her to write to them, and they usually take the photos down.

      I visit Noa every summer. We cook together, and I help her with the blog.

      Right now it is Friday afternoon, and I am sitting in Noa’s kitchen. Noa is mixing chocolate and sugar, and Maya is taking photos. I am writing this post for the blog. The kitchen smells delicious!
