      An Interview with Tom Levi

      Tom Levi is thirty-five, and he is blind. In fact, he can’t see anything at all. But Tom is an engineer, and he designs apps for people who are unable to see. Our school newspaper talks to him today.

      Do you use a lot of technology every day?
      Yes, I do! My phone reads everything to me: messages, books and the news. Technology is an enormous help for blind people.

      Does your phone help you outside, too?
      Yes, it does. When I walk in the street, the phone tells me where I am. If my direction is incorrect, it tells me, and I get back to the right street.

      What is your new app?
      It is a “smart nose”. The idea originally comes from my grandmother. She is blind too, and she loves cooking. She can’t see the food, so she uses her nose. My app smells the food and tells her when it is ready.

      Do people assume that blind people can’t work?
      Sometimes they do, and they are often surprised when they meet me. But blind people use other senses, such as hearing and touch.

      What are you doing right now?
      Right now I’m sitting in my office, and I’m testing the app. I’m also breathing in the smell of fresh coffee. For me, coffee is a natural source of energy!
