Grandpa’s Secret Soup

Omer’s grandfather is originally from Italy, and he has a small restaurant by the sea. His secret is a natural carrot soup, and he refuses to tell anyone the recipe.

One day, a big company sees the restaurant’s website and offers Grandpa an enormous deal for the recipe. A woman brings a long contract. “You’re lucky,” she says. “Don’t miss a chance like this!”

But the contract isn’t clear, and there is a fee that Grandpa must pay. “I don’t agree,” he says. Despite the money, he says no.

Right now, Grandpa is cooking in the kitchen, and Omer is helping him. There is a happy tear in Grandpa’s eye. Somehow, Omer knows that one day the secret will be his.
